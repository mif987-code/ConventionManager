The Experience

What is Spark Fest?

Spark Fest is Central America's dedicated festival celebrating Trading Card Games, Magic: The Gathering, competitive gaming culture, and the artistry behind the cards. Whether you're chasing high-stakes championship prizes or looking to jam casual Commander pods with friends, Spark Fest provides an unmatched weekend convention experience.

Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about attendance, payments, tickets, and tournaments.

How does entry and check-in work?

When you pre-register on this site, an account is prepared for you. At the convention front desk, you will receive your smart wristband linked to your account, granting you venue entry and instant event check-ins.

What currency are tickets and events priced in?

All prices on the platform are in Costa Rican Colones (CRC ₡). Online package purchases are securely processed through ONVO Pay.

What are Special Vouchers and how do they work?

Certain packages or promotions include Special Vouchers for specific categories (like On-Demand or Draft). When you register for an eligible event in the Player App, your voucher automatically covers the entry fee!

Can I unregister from an event if my schedule changes?

Yes! As long as the event has not started, you or a staff member can unregister you. If you paid with wallet credit, the colones are instantly refunded to your balance; if a voucher was used, it is restored to your profile.

How do I access the Player App?

Simply visit /app on your smartphone browser. Log in with the email and password you created during pre-registration to view your balance, vouchers, pairings, and event registrations.
